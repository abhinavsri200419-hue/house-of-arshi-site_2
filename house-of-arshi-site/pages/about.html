About House of Arshi

[Filler, replace with your story] House of Arshi is a women's fashion label born from a simple belief, that Indian women deserve a wardrobe that keeps up with them. We design for the woman who moves fast, feels deeply, and refuses to compromise style for comfort, or heritage for modernity.

From the streets of Visakhapatnam to screens across the country, House of Arshi is built to be worn, loved, and lived in, whether you are catching a 9am lecture, closing a deal in the boardroom, or winding down after a long beautiful day.

Our Beginning

When It All Started

[Filler, replace with founding year and backstory] House of Arshi was founded in [Year] in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, with a single rack of carefully curated pieces and a vision that was anything but small. What started as a passion project soon grew into a full fledged label, driven by an obsession with fit, fabric, and the feeling of putting on something that just works.

What Drives Us

Our Motive and Inspiration

[Filler, replace with brand motive] Our motive is straightforward, clothing that does not ask you to choose. Too often, Indian women feel the pull between traditional and modern, between what the occasion demands and what they actually want to wear. House of Arshi exists to dissolve that boundary.

We draw inspiration from the women around us, the college girl making her first big presentation, the working professional who wants to look sharp without looking stiff, the woman at home who deserves softness and luxury at the end of her day. Every collection is designed with all of them in mind.
